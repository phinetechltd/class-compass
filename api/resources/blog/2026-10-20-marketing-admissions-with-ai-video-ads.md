Between October and January, Kenyan parents are deciding where their children will learn next year. For PP1, Grade 1, Grade 7 and Grade 10, many families compare several schools before they commit. Most of that comparing now happens on a phone: a Facebook post shared in a church group, a TikTok of a school's music festival, a WhatsApp status from a neighbour.

Schools that show up there, with something worth watching, fill their classes first.

## What parents want to see

Before they visit, parents look for proof of a few things:

- **Results and teaching.** Not just exam numbers, but learners presenting projects, reading aloud, experimenting in a lab.
- **Safety and care.** Clean classrooms, a secure gate, a school nurse, transport.
- **Talent and activities.** Sports, music, drama, clubs. Under CBC, these matter more than ever.
- **Fees and logistics.** A clear fee structure, payment by M-Pesa, term dates and how to apply.

A 15-second video that shows one of these clearly beats a long brochure.

Many schools don't have a marketing team or a videographer. That's where AI ad tools help. You can publish them to Facebook, Instagram and TikTok, and take enquiries on WhatsApp. Plans start from KES 2,350 a month, payable by M-Pesa or card.

A simple admissions campaign for a term could be:

| Week | Ad | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Admissions open for January": a photo montage of the school with the fee range and dates | Facebook, WhatsApp Status |
| 2 | A 15-second clip of learners at a CBC project day | TikTok, Instagram Reels |
| 3 | A parent's short testimonial with a "Book a visit" button | Facebook, Instagram |
| 4 | An open-day reminder with directions and a WhatsApp number | WhatsApp, Facebook |

Use real photos of your own school, with consent from parents for any child who appears.

## Turn interest into applications

Ads only help if enquiries are handled well:

1. **Answer WhatsApp enquiries the same day.** Assign someone to respond, with a standard message giving fees, documents needed and visit times.
2. **Send every ad to one clear next step.** "Book a visit" or "Apply online" on your school website.
3. **Make the website do the work.** Your Class Compass school website already has admissions steps, a "what to bring" checklist, fees information and contact details. Link your ads straight to it.
4. **Track where families came from.** Ask on the admission form, and spend more next term on the ads that worked.

## Keep the promise after admission

The best marketing is a parent telling another parent that the school is well run: fees are easy to pay, reports arrive on time, and they always know what is happening. That part is what Class Compass is for. The ads bring families in; a well-run school keeps them and brings their friends.
