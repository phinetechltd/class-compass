Most schools still keep attendance in paper registers. They're familiar and they never run out of battery. But by the end of a term, nobody can easily answer simple questions: which learners have missed more than five days? Is Grade 4 attendance falling? Did the parent know their child was absent on Tuesday?

A digital register answers those questions automatically. Here's how to switch over in one term without disrupting teachers.

![Taking the daily register in Class Compass](/assets/classcompass/blog/admin-attendance.jpg)

## Week 1 – 2: set up and train

- **Load every class and stream** with the right learners and roll numbers. Admission data is usually already in your system; check it before day one.
- **Decide what "the register" is.** Most schools take one daily register in the morning (the first period). Some also take attendance per lesson in junior and senior school.
- **Train class teachers in a 20-minute session.** Show them how to open their class, mark everyone present with one tap, and change the few who are absent.

## Week 3 – 6: run paper and digital side by side

For a few weeks, keep the paper register as a backup while teachers get used to the digital one. Most teachers find the digital register quicker within a week, because they only touch the learners who are absent.

## Week 7 onwards: go fully digital

Once registers are being completed on time, retire the paper. Things that make it stick:

- **A deadline**, for example registers in by 8:30 a.m.
- **A daily check by the deputy** of which classes haven't submitted.
- **Absence alerts to parents.** When a learner is marked absent, the parent can get an SMS the same morning. Nothing improves attendance faster.

## What you get

After one term with a digital register, the school can see:

- **attendance for every learner**, for the month and the term, also shown to their parent;
- **class and school attendance trends** on the head teacher's dashboard;
- **learners with repeated absences**, flagged early enough to follow up;
- **accurate records** for reports, transfers and inspection.
